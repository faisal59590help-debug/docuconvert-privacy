Privacy Policy

DocuConvert – Convert Any File

Effective date: November 24, 2026
Developer: Helix Web

This Privacy Policy explains how DocuConvert handles information when you use the app. DocuConvert is a document conversion app designed to perform supported conversions on your device.

1. Information and documents you choose

DocuConvert does not ask you to create an account. When you choose a document for conversion, the app accesses the file you select using Android's file-selection system so it can perform the requested operation.

Document conversion is designed to take place locally on your device. The developer does not receive the contents of documents that you select or convert. Converted files are saved to a location you choose or to a location made available by the app.

2. Advertising and Google AdMob

DocuConvert displays advertisements using Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may collect or receive information from the app and your device to provide, measure, and improve advertising, prevent fraud, and support their services. Depending on your device settings, region, and consent choices, this information may include:

  • IP address and general location information derived from it;
  • device and advertising identifiers, where available;
  • ad views, clicks, and other advertising interactions;
  • app, device, and diagnostic information used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.

Google's handling of information is governed by Google's policies and settings. For details, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Internet connection

The supported document conversion functions are designed to work offline. An internet connection may be used to request and display advertisements and to support related advertising services. If you use an online feature in a future version, this policy will be updated to describe its data handling.

4. Sharing of information

The developer does not sell your documents or the personal information you provide to the app. Information may be collected or processed by Google AdMob and its service providers as described above, subject to Google's policies. The developer does not control Google's independent data practices.

5. Data retention and security

Your selected and converted documents remain on your device in the locations where you or the app save them. The developer does not maintain a server containing those documents. You can manage or delete saved files using the file-management features on your device.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Keep your device and its operating system updated and use the security controls available on your device.

6. Children's privacy

DocuConvert is a general-purpose utility app and is not designed specifically for children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the developer, please contact us using the details below.

7. Your choices

You can choose which documents to open and convert. You can also manage advertising and privacy settings available through your Android device and Google account. Availability of particular controls may depend on your device, region, and applicable settings.

8. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features, data practices, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The revised policy will be published at this same webpage with an updated effective date.
